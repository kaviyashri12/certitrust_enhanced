Why CertiTrust?

Built for real academic institutions that need trust without complexity

🔗

Field-Level Integrity

Every field (Name, Register No, Grades, CGPA) is hashed individually and combined into a Merkle tree. Tampering is localized instantly.

📱

Self-Verifying QR

The QR carries original field values + institution signature. No server lookup required for verification.

🛡️

Offline & Lightweight

Uses only RSA + SHA-256. No blockchain. Works for small colleges that cannot maintain heavy PKI infrastructure.

How It Works

Simple issuance. Powerful verification.

1

Enter Marks

Admin enters student details and semester grades

2

Build Merkle Tree

Each field is hashed and combined into a single root

3

Sign & QR

Root is signed with college private key and embedded in QR

4

Verify Anywhere

Anyone scans QR → signature check → exact field mismatches shown
